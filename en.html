Three missions at Caritai

Caritai was established in 2013 in fulfillment of our ongoing mission to financially, educationally and professionally support under-served college students. Since our foundation, outstanding and dedicated, service-minded professionals and academics have joined to support our mission by contributing as mentors, advisors, donators, etc.

Education, as we understand it, is not a matter of "what I can tell you," but rather, "what you can become through me." As professionals who are thankful for what we have received in life, we strive to make ourselves available to the greater community so that we might in turn invest in our students what others have invested in us.
