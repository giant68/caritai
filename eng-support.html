Join our growing community

Does our community's mission resonate with you? Do you have a passion for supporting under-served students, and improving educational equality? If so, we invite you to join our growing community.

In 2017, Caritai launched its first public fundraising campaign and exceeded its goal of CNY 15,000 (USD 2,360) through a total of 69 donations. Thanks to this generous support, we were able to double our number of scholarship recipients. This result has been a tremendous inspiration to the Caritai community, and has affirmed our commitment to our ongoing mission no matter what difficulties we face. We have no doubt that with continued public support, Caritai will be able to impact the lives of more and more students in the future.

How will Caritai continue to support students?

EXPERTISE

We will build a stronger team and apply more up-to-date research results with our practices.

FUNCTION

We will add career service to our existing support and help students connect with employers.

SCALE

We will establish partnerships with more universities.

How can you support the Caritai community?

LEAD

Offer career advice to Caritai students about trends of your industry and interview skills; Provide Caritai students with internship openings in your organization.

CONNECT

Follow Caritai's official account on WeChat to stay updated of our news; Reach out to Caritai members in your industry sectors and build new connections.

DONATE

Make a contribution to The Caritai Scholarships to secure the undergraduate education of more underprivileged and high-potential students.

PARTICIPATE

Come to join our local activities if you are in Wuhan; Sign up to share your stories at Caritai Talk if you are not in Wuhan.
